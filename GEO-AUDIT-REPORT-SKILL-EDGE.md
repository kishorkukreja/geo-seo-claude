Google and AI systems apply **heightened E-E-A-T standards** to education/training content because it falls under "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) categories — students invest significant money and time based on training provider claims. Without strong E-E-A-T signals, education sites face:
- Lower search rankings
- Exclusion from AI recommendations
- Reduced trust from prospective students

### Recommendations

1. **Accreditation is critical** — Get accredited by relevant UK bodies:
   - Ofqual (qualifications regulation)

4. **Testimonials with verification:** Named students, company names, job titles, photos
